## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Annual Report"**

Groundwork is set as a 1950s-60s American corporate identity-program annual report: warm cream uncoated paper, near-black ink, and three earth-tone spot colors (terracotta, mustard, olive) committed at field scale rather than sprinkled as accents. The page reads as a printed document with acts, folios, running heads, ruled schedules, and exhibits; the whole category's dark glowing-tech hero is deliberately refused. Material is flat ink on paper: no shadows, no gradients, no blur, no rounded corners. Density is generous and editorial: wide margins, a 1080px measure, hairline rules doing the structural work that borders and cards do elsewhere.

The world has two appearances since Aug 29, 2026 (owner-requested): **Day** (the default, described throughout this file) and the **Night Edition**, the same report read under lamplight. Night is a pure token override under `html[data-theme="night"]` (guide.css and the homepage's inline styles carry identical blocks): paper #1B1813, paper-deep #26221B, ink #F4EFE2, ink-soft #BEB4A2, rule #4E463A, terra-deep lightens to #D2802F (~5.7:1 working accent), mustard-deep #CFA032, olive #7E8F6C, olive-deep #9AA989, oxblood #C96A55; terra and mustard stay put as decorative marks. Every rule in this file (flat ink, square corners, three-weight rules, spot fields, Courier and Besley quarantines) applies unchanged at night; only the twelve tokens move. A `.modeswitch` button in every masthead toggles it (localStorage `gw-theme`, boot script in each head before paint, `theme-color` follows: #1B1813 at night). SVGs drawn with literal palette hex are routed back through the tokens by attribute-selector bridge rules in guide.css (`svg [fill="#A44E12"]{fill:var(--terra-deep)}` and friends), so every plate re-inks in both themes. Print is always Day: a print-media block resets the night tokens, so paper leaves the printer as paper.

Every value in this file is extracted from the shipped site: the homepage (`groundwork-site/index.html`, inline styles) and the shared interior stylesheet (`groundwork-site/assets/guide.css`) carry the same token set. WCAG 2.1 AA is the recorded accessibility target (per PRODUCT.md, confirmed Aug 25, 2026).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm cream paper ground with near-black warm ink; flat print material throughout.
- Spot color at section scale: an olive letter, a terra-deep audit band, terra-deep layer chips.
- Three typographic voices: Archivo grotesque (display and body), Besley slab (numerals and italic asides), Courier Prime (document file names and the Operator prompt only).
- Print devices as structure: masthead double rule, folio line, running heads, act numeral lockups, ruled ledgers and schedule tables.
- One physics: print registration — elements settle into place from offset positions.

## Colors

An earthy mid-century identity-program palette: one warm neutral family carries the page; terracotta leads, mustard and olive support, oxblood is quarantined for one meaning.

### Primary
- **Terracotta** (#C7681D, `--terra`): the program's lead spot color at decorative scale — act numerals 01/04, schedule squares, the hold-field grid lines, link underline tint, selection highlight. Never carries body text.
- **Deep Terracotta** (#A44E12, `--terra-deep`): the working form of terracotta — link hover, nav CTA fill, button hover fill, layer number chips, xref links, SVG accents and stars, the Act 05 audit band background, the caret. This is the interactive terracotta; paper text on it measures ~5.0:1.

### Secondary
- **Mustard** (#D9A419, `--mustard`): the Operator's color — the Operator disc in the cover mark and favicon. Decorative fills and large marks only.
- **Deep Mustard** (#8F6D0C, `--mustard-deep`): text-scale mustard — the Act 02 numeral. Used where mustard must sit on paper at glyph scale.

### Tertiary
- **Olive** (#5A6B4A, `--olive`): durability and Phase 3 — the covermark durability frame, the Act 03 numeral, Phase 3 schedule squares, and the full-bleed operator-letter field (paper text on olive ~5.0:1).
- **Deep Olive** (#49573C, `--olive-deep`): Phase 3 working form — Phase 3 layer chips and Phase 3 file numerals in the schedule (paper on it ~6.7:1).
- **Oxblood** (#8A2F1F, `--oxblood`): semantic only — the system's hard prohibitions: Level 4 / untrusted material in the Authority ladder, and Zone 3 / Never Automate in the governance drawing (dashed stroke, its text). Appears nowhere else.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (#F4EFE2, `--paper`): the page ground, and the text color on every spot-color field.
- **Deep Paper** (#EAE2CD, `--paper-deep`): panel tint — the hold field, exhibit mat, Operator prompt block, SVG slabs, scrollbar track, ghost-button hover.
- **Ink** (#1E1B16, `--ink`): all primary text, structural rules, button fills, scrollbar thumb, focus ring. A warm near-black, never pure #000.
- **Soft Ink** (#5C554A, `--ink-soft`, ~6.4:1 on paper): secondary text — ledes, running heads, captions, table annotations, the hold-field specks.
- **Rule** (#C9BFA8, `--rule`): hairline row dividers and dim SVG strokes only. Never text.

### Named Rules
**The Contrast Floor Rule.** Text-carrying color fields are terra-deep, olive, olive-deep, and ink — always with paper text, all ≥5:1. Interactive controls use only ink or terra-deep fills. Bright terra and mustard never carry text; they exist for large marks and decorative fills. (Sole standing exception in the build: the `::selection` highlight is terra with paper text — a transient browser surface, not a license for terra text fields.)

**The Oxblood Quarantine Rule.** Oxblood marks the system's hard prohibitions and nothing else: "untrusted / Level 4" in the Authority ladder, and "Never Automate / Zone 3" in the governance drawing. One semantic family — what the system must never treat as safe — always dashed or flagged, never decorative. (Amended 2026-08-26 to record the Zone 3 use carried over from the owner-approved Visual Wave.)

**The Spot-Field Rule.** Spot color commits at section scale (the olive letter, the terra-deep audit band) or at device scale (chips, squares, numerals). It is not used for tinted backgrounds behind ordinary prose.

**The Alias Bridge Rule.** `:root` retains legacy alias custom properties (`--canvas`, `--panel`, `--accent`, `--accent-hover`, `--accent-muted`, `--text-primary`, `--text-secondary`, `--btn-ink`) mapped onto the new tokens so inline SVG `var()` references inherit the world. Keep these aliases when porting pages that carry inline SVG; write new CSS against the new token names.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo (with Arial, 'Helvetica Neue' fallback) — weights 500, 600, 700, 900
**Body Font:** Archivo (same stack) — body sits at weight 500; strong is 700
**Slab Font:** Besley (with Georgia fallback) — 400, 700, 900 upright; 400, 600 italic
**Mono Font:** Courier Prime (with 'Courier New', ui-monospace fallback) — 400, 700

**Character:** A grotesque doing the corporate speaking, a slab doing the numbering and the human asides, a typewriter face reserved for the documents themselves. Display setting is heavy (900), tight (-0.02em), and uppercase at h1/h2; labels are small, bold, and widely tracked (0.14-0.24em) uppercase.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (900, clamp(2.6rem, 5vw, 4.1rem) since Sept 5, 2026, lh 1.06, ls -0.02em, uppercase): the cover headline only; max-width 22ch, which holds four lines at desktop widths so the ledger and both buttons clear a 900px fold.
- **Headline** (900, clamp(1.7rem, 3.2vw, 2.6rem), lh 1.06, uppercase): section h2, max-width 24ch. Secondary sections (Curriculum, Compare, FAQ) step h2 down to clamp(1.3rem, 2.3vw, 1.75rem) in soft ink.
- **Title** (700, 1.5rem, lh 1.06, ls -0.01em): h3, e.g. layer names.
- **Body** (500, 17px, lh 1.62): all prose; ledes and secondary prose in soft ink; measures capped at 52-64ch.
- **Label** (700, 0.58-0.78rem, ls 0.14-0.24em, uppercase, Archivo): folio, running heads, contents strip, table headers, SVG tags. All "technical-looking" UI labels are Archivo bold caps, not mono.
- **Numeral** (Besley 900, clamp(4.2rem, 9vw, 7.5rem), lh 0.82): act numerals in spot color; also at 2.6rem in layer chips and 1.1rem in argument rows.
- **Aside** (Besley italic, 400-600, ~1.05-1.5rem): the cover sub, layer promises, pull quotes, "the Operator" annotation, the letter signature — the human voice inside the corporate document.
- **File** (Courier Prime, 0.85-0.86rem): see the rule below.

### Named Rules
**The Courier Quarantine Rule.** Courier Prime appears in exactly two places: the fifteen document file names in the schedule table, and the Operator prompt block. Nothing else — labels, tags, and numbers that look technical are Archivo 700 caps.

**The Besley Voice Rule.** Besley carries numerals (900 upright) and the human register (italic asides, promises, pull quotes, signatures). It never sets headings, UI labels, or body prose.

## Layout

The page is one column of full-width sections on paper, each padded `clamp(3.8rem, 9vh, 7rem)` vertical by `clamp(1.2rem, 6vw, 4rem)` horizontal. Content sits in a 1080px `wrap`; prose-led sections use a 760px `narrow` measure. Structure is drawn, not boxed: sections open with a running head set into a 1px ink top rule; major lists open with a 2px ink rule; rows divide with 1px `--rule` hairlines; the last row drops its rule.

Recurring document devices: the masthead double rule (nav: 2px ink border-bottom plus an offset 1px line; the footer mirrors it top-side), the folio line (justified caption row under the masthead, bordered below), the contents strip (2px top / 1px bottom rules, act numbers in Besley terra-deep), act lockups (numeral column + heading grid, `auto 1fr`, collapsing to one column at 640px), and the exhibit mat (a framed figure between sections).

Grids are two-column and collapse early: cover 1.15fr/0.85fr (→ 1 col at 860px), quote ledger and entries 1fr/1.2fr (→ 680px), layers/argument `auto 1fr` (→ 560px/640px). The nav collapses to a bordered MENU toggle at 720px. Wide diagrams scroll horizontally inside their section below 680px, labeled "Continued across → drag to read". The schedule table stacks its rows below 680px (thead hidden); the compare table stacks with `data-h` labels injected as headings. Touch targets: buttons and nav links get 44px min-height on coarse pointers.

## Elevation & Depth

None. The material is flat ink on uncoated paper: zero `box-shadow`, zero gradients, zero blur anywhere in the build. Depth and hierarchy are conveyed by rule weight (2px openings vs 1px hairlines), the two-step paper tint (`--paper` ground, `--paper-deep` panels bounded by 1px ink borders), and full spot-color fields. In the SVG diagram language, de-emphasis is drawn the same way: dim slabs take `--rule` strokes instead of ink, and reduced stroke-width stands in for reduced trust.

### Named Rules
**The Flat Ink Rule.** Nothing floats. If an element needs separation, it gets a rule, a `--paper-deep` fill with a 1px ink border, or a spot-color field — never a shadow, glow, or gradient.

## Shapes

Square everything: `border-radius: 0` on every box, button, chip, panel, and table. The only circles are meaningful marks — the mustard Operator disc, the journey-stop and star dots in diagrams, and the hold-field ink specks. Border language is a three-weight system: 2px ink for structure (masthead, buttons, section openings), 1px ink for containers (panels, mats, outcome chips), 1px `--rule` for hairline dividers — plus one semantic stroke, dashed oxblood, for untrusted material. The brand mark (favicon and nav/footer) is three terracotta bars stacked over an ink ground line with the mustard Operator disc above.

### Named Rules
**The Printed Edge Rule.** Corners are square because print is square. A circle is never decoration; it is either the Operator or a plotted point.

- A `--paper-deep` field (1px ink border) of 24 soft-ink specks scattered by seeded RNG; press-and-hold on the button below (its terra-deep fill scales X with progress) interpolates every speck onto a 6×4 ruled grid over 2 seconds while terra grid lines fade in; released early, order decays; completed, the specks set terra-deep and stay. Keyboard operable (Space/Enter). "Order is made, not bought," enacted.

### Themed Browser Surfaces
- Selection: terra highlight, paper text (paper/terra-deep on the audit band). Scrollbar: ink thumb with 3px `--paper-deep` border on a `--paper-deep` track, 12px (plus `scrollbar-color`). Focus: 2px solid ink outline, offset 3px. Caret: terra-deep. `theme-color`: paper. Skip link: ink chip, paper text.

### Motion
- **One physics: print registration.** Things settle into place from offset positions. The cover mark's plates translate in from per-plate offsets (`register`, 0.9s `--ease-out`, staggered 0.1-0.62s). ## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** open every section with the running head device: a 1px ink top rule with a right-aligned tracked-caps label (paper-on-spot fields switch both to paper).
- **Do** draw structure with the three-weight rule system: 2px ink to open, 1px ink to contain, 1px `--rule` (#C9BFA8) to divide rows; drop the rule on the last row.
- **Do** keep paper text on terra-deep, olive, olive-deep, or ink fields only — the AA floor the build ships (~5:1 minimum).
- **Do** keep the legacy alias custom properties in `:root` on any page carrying inline SVG `var()` references; write new styles against the new token names.
- **Do** use the one physics for every entrance: settle-into-register (offset → 0, `--ease-out`), staggered under ~0.1s per part, with reduced-motion pinning final states.
- **Do** give buttons and nav links 44px minimum touch targets on coarse pointers.
- **Do** cap prose measures (52-64ch) and let hairline-ruled whitespace, not boxes, do the separating.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use shadows, gradients, glows, or blur in either theme — the material is flat ink on paper. Dark surfaces exist only as the Night Edition token override (see Overview); never hand-pick a dark background outside those tokens.
- **Don't** round a corner. Radius is 0 everywhere; circles are reserved for the Operator disc and plotted marks.
- **Don't** set text on bright terra (#C7681D) or mustard (#D9A419) fields; they are large-mark and decorative-fill colors (the selection highlight is the lone shipped exception).
- **Don't** use Courier Prime outside the fifteen file names and the Operator prompt; technical-looking labels are Archivo 700 tracked caps.
- **Don't** use oxblood outside its prohibition semantics: untrusted/Level 4 and Never Automate/Zone 3.
- **Don't** introduce new accent hues, pure black, or pure white; the palette is the twelve tokens above.
