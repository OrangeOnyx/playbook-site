Department Deep-Dives

Every department gets a 3-hour deep-dive facilitated by its AI Champion, twice a year. Attendance is expected.

The template

Every deep-dive follows this structure:

Hour 1 — What we're using now

  • Live demo: the top 5 workflows in this department
  • Show real inputs, real outputs, real time saved
  • Metrics: adoption %, deflection %, quality scores

Hour 2 — What's new

  • New models (bake-off results)
  • New tools approved for this department
  • New workflows in beta
  • What other departments have built that this one could adapt

Hour 3 — Hands-on

  • Split into pairs
  • Each pair picks a real backlog task and tries to build a workflow for it
  • Present to group
  • Best 1-2 candidates go to Champion for productionization

Per-department playbooks

Sales

Focus areas: - Prospecting research (single-lead + wide-search) - Meeting prep (industry, company, personas) - Call transcript → CRM extraction - Follow-up drafting - Proposal / SOW drafting - Competitive positioning

Support

Focus areas: - Ticket triage - Response drafting - KB article maintenance - Escalation summaries - Customer sentiment analysis - Root cause categorization

Legal

Focus areas: - Contract review (redlines) - Clause extraction and comparison - NDA fast track - Regulatory monitoring - Meeting brief prep - Response templates

Finance

Focus areas: - Variance analysis - Reconciliation prep - Vendor contract review - Board-deck drafting - Ad-hoc data analysis - Automation of month-end tasks

Engineering

Focus areas: - Code review - Test generation - Doc generation - Bug diagnosis - Refactor planning - Design doc drafting

Marketing

Focus areas: - Content generation (posts, emails, ads) - Voice-of-customer synthesis - Campaign brief drafting - SEO planning - Competitive teardowns - Brand-voice compliance

HR

Focus areas: - Job description drafting - Interview kit generation - Feedback synthesis - Policy Q&A - Onboarding materials - Performance-review support

Executive

Focus areas: - Board-deck drafting - Weekly / monthly report synthesis - Strategic decision prep - Meeting brief prep - Long-form writing - Scenario planning

Operations

Focus areas: - Process documentation - Vendor evaluation - SOP drafting - Data reconciliation - Meeting summaries - Cross-team briefings

Champion responsibilities

For each deep-dive, the department AI Champion:

  1. Books the session 3 weeks out
  2. Collects demo material (recent wins, current workflows, backlog items)
  3. Runs the session
  4. Documents outcomes: workflows to build, workflows to promote, workflows to retire
  5. Reports to Steering Committee within 1 week

Outcomes tracked

For each deep-dive: - Attendance % - Number of new workflows identified - Number of workflows promoted to production within 30 days - Champion confidence score (survey) - Team confidence score (survey)

Onboarding Curriculum — New Employee AI Training

Every new employee completes this within their first 30 days. Structured as five 45-minute modules.

Module 1 — What AI can and can't do here (Day 1)

Objectives: - Understand what tools we've approved - Know what the tools do well and what they don't - Know our data classification rules

Content: - Tour of the approved AI tools (chat, IDE, meeting assistant, department copilot) - The 4-tier data classification: Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted - The tool tiers: which tools take which classes - Live demo: routing an example task to the right tool

Assessment: - 10-question quiz on data classification - Must pass 90% to continue

Module 2 — Your first workflow (Week 1)

Objectives: - Compose a good prompt - Understand system prompts vs user prompts - Use RAG (department copilot) effectively

Content: - The 4-part prompt: Role, Task, Constraints, Format - Live-write a prompt for a real task with the trainer - How to use the department copilot: when to search KB vs when to reason - How to iterate: what to do when the answer is wrong

Assessment: - Complete 3 real tasks with AI assistance - Submit prompts + outputs to trainer for review

Module 3 — Personal setup (Week 1)

Objectives: - Configure your master system prompt - Load the About Me block - Set memory hygiene defaults

Content: - Work through individual/03-system-prompts/ templates - Configure ChatGPT Custom Instructions - Configure Claude Project settings - Configure Perplexity Spaces - Configure Cursor / Copilot personal preferences

Assessment: - Show your master system prompt to the trainer - Show your Custom Instructions - Demonstrate memory is set correctly

Module 4 — Guardrails and safety (Week 2)

Objectives: - Know what NOT to paste into AI - Know how to spot a hallucination - Know how to report incidents

Content: - Data classification examples — role-play "would you paste this?" - Hallucination examples: fabricated citations, wrong facts, made-up features - Verification patterns: cross-check, ask for sources, use grounded tools - Incident reporting: what, when, how, to whom

Assessment: - Complete 5 "spot the problem" case studies - Discuss with trainer

Module 5 — Your department's playbook (Week 3)

Objectives: - Deep dive on your department's copilot - Learn your department's top 5 AI workflows - Meet your AI Champion

Content: - Facilitated by department AI Champion - Walk through the department copilot's prompt library - Practice with 3 real recent tasks - Q&A

Assessment: - Ship 1 real work output using AI in your first week post-training - Peer or manager reviews the output

Continuing education

After the 5 modules: - Weekly "AI office hours" (30 min, opt-in) - Monthly "what shipped" showcase across the company - Quarterly deep-dive on a specific topic - Certifications for advanced users (see quarterly-refresh)

Trainer materials

  • Slide deck (in company Drive)
  • Recordings of past sessions
  • FAQ document
  • Exercise workbook
  • Assessment answer keys

Owner: L&D + AI Steering Committee jointly.

Quarterly AI Refresh — All Employees

Every quarter, every employee attends a 60-minute refresh. This is how you keep pace with the field.

Why

New models drop. New capabilities emerge. Old habits go stale. Without a forcing function, employees stop improving.

Structure

Segment 1 — What's new in the field (15 min)

  • Big model releases this quarter
  • New capabilities: vision, voice, tool use, longer context
  • Notable industry shifts

Segment 2 — What's new in our stack (15 min)

  • Tools added, tools removed
  • Policy updates
  • New workflows launched
  • Retired workflows and why

Segment 3 — Team wins (15 min)

  • 3-5 short spotlights from different departments
  • Real workflow, real impact, real numbers
  • Q&A with the builder

Segment 4 — What's next quarter (15 min)

  • Roadmap for the AI program
  • Upcoming trainings
  • New tools in evaluation
  • Requests for pilots

Owned by

  • Steering Committee (agenda + guest list)
  • AI Champions (spotlight preparation)
  • L&D (logistics)

Certifications

Employees who complete the quarterly refresh for 4 consecutive quarters earn an internal "AI Power User" cert. Bonus: paired with department Champion for advanced training.

Advanced tracks

Beyond the quarterly, three optional certification tracks:

Prompt Engineer track

  • 6-week structured curriculum
  • Advanced prompting patterns
  • Eval design
  • Cost optimization

Agent Builder track

  • 8-week hands-on
  • MCP server construction
  • Agent workflow design
  • Safety engineering

AI Product Manager track

  • 10-week curriculum
  • Metrics, adoption, ROI
  • Roadmap and prioritization
  • Cross-functional coordination

Certifications are internally recognized and may be tied to promotion criteria per role.

Refresh recording

Every refresh is recorded. Employees who miss the live session must watch within 2 weeks.

Feedback loop

After every refresh: - 5-question survey (usefulness, pace, requests for next time) - Champion syncs to review feedback - Adjustments feed next quarter's agenda
